Common Questions

Parent Questions

What age can my child start?
We welcome kids ages 5–12, split into a Little Kids (5–7) group and a Kids (8–12) group so every child trains with peers their own age and size.
Does my child need any experience?
Not at all. Most kids start with zero martial arts background, and beginners can join at any time.
Will my child get hurt?
Jiu-jitsu is grappling-based, not striking — no punching or kicking. Kids learn through age-appropriate games and controlled, supervised drilling with experienced coaches.
What should my child wear and bring?
For the free class, comfortable athletic clothes (a t-shirt and shorts or leggings) and a water bottle are all your child needs.
Is the first class really free?
Yes. Your child's first kids class is completely free — no enrollment fee and no obligation.
How often are kids classes?
Three times a week. Kids 8–12 train Tuesday and Thursday at 4:30 PM and Saturday at 10:15 AM. Little Kids 5–7 train Tuesday and Thursday at 5:30 PM and Saturday at 11:15 AM.
